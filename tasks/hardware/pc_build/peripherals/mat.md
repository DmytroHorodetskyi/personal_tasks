# Desk mat / mouse pad

**Status: closed 2026-09-11 — A4Tech X7-300MP (4711421735193), 299 UAH. Confirmed by Dmytro.**
He stated the basic option is acceptable, which settles the only open question: the mat
covers the mouse, not the desk. He then proposed two cheaper mats himself; one of them beats
the HATOR this file had picked, on size *and* price, and became the answer.

No size was ever specified. The earlier version of this research priced only XXL pads
(1 566-2 299) because the 2026-09-09 note happened to say "XXL" — that was an assumption, not
a requirement, and it made the whole category look four times more expensive than it is. **A
perfectly good mat costs 169-799.**

## The characteristics that matter, and how to read them

| # | Spec (unit) | What it means, plainly | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Size (mm)** | Medium ~360x270, Large ~450x400, XL ~900x300, XXL ~900x400, and "deskmat" ~1200x600. | **The only spec that changes the experience.** It is set by the desk, not by the mouse. |
| 2 | **Surface** | Cloth (almost everything here) vs hard plastic/glass. | Cloth is the default for a reason: quiet, forgiving, cheap. Hard pads are a competitive-FPS taste. |
| 3 | **Speed vs control weave** | "Control" cloth has more friction, "speed" less. | A real difference, a small one. Unbranded "control" is the safe default. |
| 4 | **Thickness (mm)** | 2-3 mm standard, 4-6 mm "Heavy". | Thicker = more wrist cushioning and it hides an uneven desk surface. |
| 5 | **Stitched edge** | A sewn border stops the edge fraying. | The difference between a mat that lasts two years and five. **Correction 2026-09-11:** this file said "present on everything above ~500" — the 449 Tonn Evo M has it too, confirmed on Foxtrot's own listing. |
| 6 | **Base** | Rubber grip underneath. | Universal at every price here. Not a differentiator. |

**Ignore:** RGB borders, "esports team edition" branding, and per-cm² price comparisons
between sizes — a bigger pad is not better value, it is a different product.

## What the table says

- **This category has no quality ladder, only a size ladder.** A 2 199 cloth pad does not
  glide better than a 799 one. Anything above ~800 is being paid for area and a brand name.
- **Mouse-sized is 169-1 099.** If the mat is only there for the mouse, the Gigantus V2
  Medium at 799 or the Hator Tonn Evo M at 449 end the question.
- **Full-desk coverage starts at 1 599** — the DM Pad XXL at 1200x600 is the cheapest thing
  that puts keyboard, mouse and both forearms on one surface, and it undercuts the 900x400
  SteelSeries and Razer pads that cover far less.
- **Rozetka is the wrong shop for this.** QcK XXL is 2 039 there against 1 899 at Telemart.

## The verdict

Revised 2026-09-11 on Dmytro's own suggestion. The earlier pick was the HATOR Tonn Evo M at
449; the A4Tech is larger, equally well made and 150 cheaper, so it replaces it.

**A4Tech X7-300MP (4711421735193), 299 UAH — Telemart, Brain or ITbox.** 437 x 350 x 3 mm,
cloth over natural rubber, **stitched edges**, in stock at all three read direct.

| | **A4Tech X7-300MP** | Hator Tonn Evo M | SHLab SHM-846 |
|---|---|---|---|
| | **the pick** | previous pick | rejected |
| Size | **437 x 350 x 3 mm** | 360 x 300 x 4 mm | 900 x 400, *see below* |
| Stitched edge | yes | yes | claimed |
| UAH | **299** | 449 | ~399 on Prom |
| Stocked at a shop read direct | **Telemart, Brain, ITbox** | Brain, Foxtrot, Telemart | **none of the four** |

It is 77 x 50 mm larger than the HATOR for 150 less, with the same cloth-over-rubber build and
the same stitched edge. The single spec it loses on is thickness, 3 mm against 4 — which
matters only on an uneven desk. Ukrainian user reviews report it intact after a year.

**Why the SHLab SHM-846 is rejected, and it is not about the reviews:** none of the four shops
read direct carries it. It exists on Prom.ua, Rozetka marketplace sellers and Epicentr — that
is, third-party sellers without a service department, which is exactly the case
[../price_check.md](../price_check.md) was written against. Second, the same article number
**SHM-846 is listed at 70x30, 70x40 and 90x40 cm** by different sellers, so the size cannot be
established before it arrives. A mat is cheap enough that neither point is a disaster; but
there is no reason to take an unverifiable listing when 299 buys a verified one.

**Cheaper:** nothing verified below 299 keeps the stitched edge and a published size.

**Pricier: Hator Tonn eSport L (HTP-032), 699 at Foxtrot** — 500 x 420 mm. Worth it only for
low-sensitivity arm-sweep aiming, which is not what this machine is for.

**Closed:** how much of the desk it covers — mouse only, per Dmytro 2026-09-11. Full-desk
coverage (1 599+) and the 900x400 class (1 849-2 199 branded) are out of scope.
