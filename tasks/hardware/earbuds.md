## What actually decides an earbud, for this use

The category is sold on microphone counts and codec logos, neither of which predicts how it
performs. What follows is ordered by how much it matters **here**, not in general.

Each row gives the scale the number lives on, the line between bad and good, and the target for
this purchase. **"Judgement line" marks a threshold drawn from reviewer consensus rather than a
single published measurement** - the distinction matters when a shop quotes you a figure.

| # | Spec | Unit and range | Bad / Good / Excellent | Target here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Microphone - recording quality** | No standard unit; RTINGS scores 0-10. The physical proxy is the bandwidth the mic passes: a poor one rolls off below 300 Hz and above 4 kHz, a good one carries **100 Hz - 7 kHz**. | Judgement line: thin and nasal / natural but compressed / indistinguishable from the phone's own mic | **Top of the list.** Listen to a voice sample before paying - every serious outlet publishes one. |
| 2 | **Microphone - noise handling** | The suppression applied to background, in dB, and whether the gate takes your voice with it. Earbud mics sit 10-15 cm from the mouth against 2-3 cm for a boom mic, so they are fighting a ~15 dB worse starting signal-to-noise. | Judgement line: voice cuts out in wind / voice survives but thins / voice stable through traffic and wind | **The second deciding spec**, and the one that separated the shortlist in testing. |
| 3 | **ANC depth** | Attenuation in **dB**, strongly frequency-dependent. ANC works below ~1 kHz and does almost nothing above it. At **100 Hz** (engine and traffic rumble): weak 5-10 dB, mid-range 12-20 dB, flagship **20-30 dB**. Every 10 dB is roughly a halving of perceived loudness. | <10 dB / 15-20 dB / 25-30 dB at 100 Hz | **15-20 dB is enough.** ANC was declared nice-to-have. Do not pay 5 000 for the last 8 dB. |
| 4 | **Passive isolation** | The seal, in dB, and it is what handles everything **above 1 kHz** - voices, keyboards, traffic hiss. Typically **10-30 dB** depending almost entirely on tip fit. | Judgement line: loose tip / correct tip / correct tip plus foam | Matters in both directions on a call - it is how *you* hear the caller. A wrong tip size costs more dB than a whole ANC generation. |
| 5 | **Latency** | **Milliseconds**, end to end. SBC 150-250, AAC on Android 150-250 and variable, aptX 80-120, aptX Adaptive 50-80, **LDAC 200-300**, LE Audio / LC3 20-50. Low-latency and game modes land at 50-90. | >150 ms clearly out of sync / 45-100 ms noticeable to some / <45 ms imperceptible | **Under ~100 ms**, or a low-latency mode you can switch on. Note that **LDAC is the worst offender** - the codec that helps music hurts video. |
| 6 | **Codec** | Music bitrate, **kbps**: SBC 328, AAC 256, aptX 384, aptX Adaptive 279-420, **LDAC 330 / 660 / 990**, LHDC 400-1000. | - | **Irrelevant to calls.** A call drops to mSBC at ~64 kbps or LC3 at 32-64 kbps whatever the box says - so the codec logo describes music only. The Xiaomi 14T Pro does LDAC and LHDC, making this a free bonus, never a reason to choose. |
| 7 | **Battery, ANC on** | **Hours** per charge, then total with the case. Budget 4-6 h, mid-range 5-7 h, flagship 7-9 h; the case adds 2-4 charges for **20-40 h** total. Marketing quotes the ANC-**off** figure, which runs 20-30% higher. | <4 h / 5-7 h / 8 h+ | **5 h+** covers a day of calls. Measured on the shortlist: OnePlus ~5 h, CMF 6 h+, Liberty 5 Pro 6.5-6.9 h. Expect ~20% capacity loss after ~500 cycles, so roughly two years. |
| 8 | **Weight per bud** | **Grams.** Typical 4-6 g. Above ~6 g the bud works loose during talking and chewing, which is exactly when you need it not to. | >6.5 g / 5-6 g / <5 g | Under 6 g, or a wing/fin that locks it in. Reviewers repeatedly report the heavy Sony models falling out. |
| 9 | **Multipoint** | Number of simultaneous devices: **1, 2 or 3**. | 1 / 2 / 3 | 2 is plenty - the desk machine has its own headphones. Three-way is a Liberty 5 Pro exclusive on this list. |
| 10 | **IP rating** | IPX4 = splash and sweat. IP54 = dust plus splash. IP55/IP57 = jets or immersion. | none / IPX4 / IP54+ | **IPX4 is enough.** Not a differentiator for this use. |
| 11 | **Fast charge** | **Minutes in the case per hour of playback.** Typical: 10 min gives 1-2 h. | - | Nice to have, decides nothing. |
| 12 | **App** | EQ bands (3 / 5 / 8 / parametric), ANC modes, firmware, mic mute. | 3-band / 5-band / parametric | All shortlisted have one. Some features are locked to the maker's own phone - OnePlus' AI Translation needs a OnePlus handset. |

**One number that works in your favour:** a GSM call is capped at **300 Hz - 3.4 kHz** narrowband,
or 50 Hz - 7 kHz on HD Voice. Telegram and similar apps use Opus and carry **much wider audio than
the dialler does**. So the microphone matters *more* in the apps you named than in a phone call -
a mic that rolls off at 4 kHz will sound acceptable on a GSM call and obviously poor on Telegram.

**Ignore:** driver diameter in millimetres, "Hi-Res Audio Wireless" stickers, advertised
microphone counts, and total battery figures that add the case.

Dmytro asked whether the Pro 4-over-Pro 5 verdict rested on one source. **It did.** Six sources

**RTINGS has never reviewed the FreeBuds Pro 4** (the URL 404s), so the older model has **no lab
measurement at all** - neither for nor against.

**What survives cross-validation, stated exactly:**

1. **Three independent reviewers rate the Pro 4's microphone at the top of its class**, and the one
   that heard both prefers it to the Pro 5. That part holds.
2. **The Pro 5's microphone is disputed, not established.** Four listening reviews praise it; the
   only lab that measures microphones calls it poor in background noise - which is the condition
   this purchase cares about.
3. **Neither Huawei has a positive lab measurement.** If a measured microphone is the requirement,
   neither qualifies; RTINGS' own picks for calls at this budget are the **CMF Buds Pro 2**
   (budget) and **OnePlus Buds 4** (mid-range), and the OnePlus is withdrawn here on the MediaTek
   sound finding.
4. **The battery question is settled, and against the Pro 5:** RTINGS measured **5.5 h**
   continuous, which is the low end of the two figures previously on file and below the 6.5 h
   MyChooz reports for the Pro 4.

**The recommendation does not change - buy the Pro 4 at 7 299 - but the reason narrows.** It is
not "the Pro 4 measures better"; nothing measures either. It is that the Pro 4 is **700 cheaper**,
carries **three** top-of-class microphone verdicts to the Pro 5's contested one, has the **longer
measured battery**, and loses only fuller bass and a *"marginal"* ANC gain.
