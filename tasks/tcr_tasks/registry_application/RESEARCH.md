# Заява to the ТЦК about data in the Реєстр — rules as of 2026-09-08

Working notes, not legal advice. Every rule carries its act and date; the governing acts here were
replaced twice in 2025–2026, so re-verify anything older than a few weeks.

---

## 1. The routes — and which one this template actually is

A бронювання missing from «Оберіг» has **three distinct routes**, and they are not alternatives to
choose between: they are a sequence. The template is route 3.

| # | Route | When it is the right one |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Employer → Дія → Мінекономіки** | Almost always first. Бронювання data does not originate at the ТЦК: the employer submits it through Дія, Мінекономіки maintains the Єдиний перелік, and the result propagates to the employer's Дія cabinet and to Резерв+. A missing entry is usually a defect upstream of the ТЦК — a wrong РНОКПП, date of birth or employment record in the employer's submission. Start at the кадрова служба; they can see the Дія result and escalate to the service's support. |
| 2 | **Резерв+ → «Виправити дані онлайн»** | For contact details, personal data and employment data the app exposes. It will not create a бронювання record. Worth doing because it is free and instant, and because a rejection there is evidence for route 3. |
| 3 | **Заява to the орган ведення Реєстру (this template)** | When routes 1–2 do not fix it: the upstream data is correct, and the Реєстр still does not hold it. Also the mandatory paper trail before any litigation — the courts want to see that the ТЦК was asked and did not act. |

Escalation beyond route 3, if it is ignored: адвокатський запит, then an адміністративний позов.
Courts have held that a ТЦК must not only take the decision but **also enter the data in
«Оберіг»**, that the burden cannot be shifted onto the citizen, and that technical problems in the
register do not excuse inaction ([НААУ, судова практика](https://unba.org.ua/publications/10491-yak-zmusiti-tck-onoviti-dani-v-reestri-oberig-sudova-praktika.html)).

## 2. Templates: what else exists, and what conflicts with this one

### 2.1 There is no official form — and that is the finding

The right to file exists in **ст. 9 ч. 1 п. 2 Закону України «Про Єдиний державний реєстр
призовників, військовозобов'язаних та резервістів» № 1951-VIII**: the person may apply
«з мотивованою заявою щодо неправомірного включення (невключення) до Реєстру запису про себе,
виправлення недостовірних відомостей Реєстру» — in the order established by the register's
administrator.

The current order is **Порядок, затверджений постановою КМУ від 29.07.2026 № 981** (in force
08.08.2026; it replaced постанови № 523 від 10.05.2024, № 704 від 17.06.2024 and № 1180 від
15.10.2024). Its **п. 43** provides:

> «Виправлення недостовірних відомостей Реєстру, зміна облікових даних, а також включення до
> Реєстру чи виключення з нього … здійснюються за результатами розгляду відповідної мотивованої
> заяви …»

**It prescribes no form, no template and no mandatory attachment list.** So a free-form заява is
correct, and the template's shape — addressee, applicant's details, facts, legal grounds, ПРОШУ,
ДОДАТКИ, date, signature — is the right shape. The word that carries weight is **мотивована**: the
facts and the legal basis have to be in the document, which is why the long quotations in the
template belong there rather than being trimmed.

### 2.2 Forms that look applicable but are not

- **Додаток 4 до Порядку № 1487** (повідомлення про зміну облікових даних). Commonly reached for,
  and **wrong here on two counts**: it is submitted by the *employer*, not the person, and
  бронювання is explicitly outside its grounds — «як раніше, так і після змін не подаємо додаток
  4 … при бронюванні чи розбронюванні». Its form was restated from 16.12.2025 and its grounds
  narrowed further from 27.06.2026 (now: ПІБ, passport details, address, place of work, position).
- **Заява про відстрочку через Резерв+.** A different instrument: it *requests* a відстрочка. Here
  the відстрочка already exists and is merely unrecorded — filing this instead concedes the point.
- **Заява про виправлення даних у Резерв+** (the template circulating on legal-aid sites). Same
  legal basis as ours, but drafted for personal-data errors (name, РНОКПП, ВЛК status). Usable as a
  cross-check on wording; it does not cover бронювання.

### 2.3 What the template misses that the current law gives you

The template's legal argument rests only on the Закон. Two more acts are directly on point and
strengthen it considerably:

- **Порядок, затверджений постановою КМУ від 16.05.2024 № 559** (оформлення та видача ВОД; amended
  by постанова № 1644 від 10.12.2025, changes in force 16.12.2025). **П. 8** requires the
  electronic ВОД to carry «відомості про наявність відстрочки … від призову на військову службу
  під час мобілізації на особливий період (бронювання)». This converts the complaint from "my
  record is incomplete" into "the document the State issues me is defective by its own rules".
- **П. 4** of the same Порядок: to have such data changed the citizen uses the Portal or applies in
  person to the district (city) ТЦК та СП, and **the change is made within five working days**.

## 3. Deadlines to cite and to hold them to

| Deadline | Source | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **5 робочих днів** from registration of the заява | Порядок № 559, п. 4 (corroborated by practitioner commentary on Порядок № 981) | Actually entering the data |
| **up to 30 днів**, and «невідкладно, але не пізніше 15 днів» where no additional study is needed | ст. 20 Закону України «Про звернення громадян» № 393/96-ВР | The formal written reply |

Cite the five working days in the заява. The 30-day term is what they will fall back on; it is the
clock that matters for the "inaction" element if this goes further.

## 4. How to fill it — and the defects to fix first

### 4.1 Defects in the current template

1. **The quoted law is out of date.** The template quotes «8. Органами ведення Реєстру є районні
   … територіальні центри комплектування …». Since Закон № 4720-IX від 16.12.2025 that provision
   (ст. 5 ч. 8) also names **Міністерство оборони України** and **центри рекрутингу Збройних Сил
   України**. Quoting the superseded wording is the kind of thing an unwilling clerk uses to set the
   заява aside. Re-copy both quoted articles from `zakon.rada.gov.ua` on the day of filing.
2. **The date line says «2024 року».** Fix to 2026.
3. **The addressee is wrong for this заява.** The template addresses «Керівнику РТЦК та СП» — the
   *regional* centre. The орган ведення Реєстру is the **district / city (районний, міський) ТЦК та
   СП** where the person is on accounting (Порядок № 981, п. 24; ст. 5 ч. 8 Закону № 1951-VIII).
   Address it there; a copy to the regional centre is a choice, not a substitute.
4. **`ДОДАТКИ:` is empty.** An unmotivated заява with no evidence is the most common reason for a
   formal brush-off. See §4.3.
5. **The «ПРОШУ» is too thin.** «додати інформацію щодо мого бронювання» leaves the ТЦК free to
   answer that it "took it into account". Ask for the three separate acts in §4.2.

### 4.2 What the ПРОШУ should demand

Three numbered requests, so that a partial answer is visibly partial:

1. Enter into the Реєстр the data on the бронювання and the resulting відстрочка від призову during
   mobilisation, with its period and its ground (the витяг and the employer's order, named by number
   and date).
2. Re-form (or confirm the possibility of forming) the електронний військово-обліковий документ
   **with** the відстрочка shown, per п. 8 Порядку № 559.
3. Inform the applicant in writing of what was entered, within the terms of п. 4 Порядку № 559 and
   ст. 20 Закону № 393/96-ВР, to the postal and electronic addresses given.

### 4.3 What to attach (`ДОДАТКИ`)

Number them in the заява and mirror the numbering on the copies. The set: passport / ID and РНОКПП;
the військово-обліковий документ (or a screenshot of the electronic one showing no відстрочка —
this *is* the evidence of the defect, so date it); the витяг Мінекономіки with its number and date;
the employer's order on the бронювання; a certificate from the employer that the person is employed
and booked as of the current date; screenshots from Резерв+ showing the бронювання both when it was
visible and now that it is not; and, if routes 1–2 were tried, whatever came back from them.

For each attachment state «копія на N арк.». Do not surrender originals.

### 4.4 Formal requirements of the заява itself

From **ст. 5 Закону № 393/96-ВР**, all of which the template's header already satisfies — verify
none of it is left blank:

- прізвище, ім'я, по батькові;
- місце проживання (the template's split into «адреса проживання» and «адреса для листування» is
  correct and useful — the reply goes to the second);
- the substance of the matter and the request;
- **the applicant's signature and the date** — a written звернення without both may be returned;
- for an electronic filing, an e-mail address for the reply.

An **electronic** звернення under ст. 5 does not require a КЕП. A КЕП is nonetheless worth using
where possible, because it removes the "we cannot identify the sender" answer.

Keep the Ukrainian prose plain and impersonal, and run it through `/style-check` before printing.

## 5. Filing it, and proving you filed it

Three lawful channels: **in person**, **by post**, **by e-mail** (ст. 5 Закону № 393/96-ВР).
The proof of filing matters more than the channel, because everything downstream is built on it:

- **In person** — bring **two copies**. One is filed; on the second insist on the вхідний stamp
  with its number and date. That stamped copy is the document a court is shown.
- **By post** — рекомендованим листом **з описом вкладення та повідомленням про вручення**. Keep
  the опис, the receipt and the returned повідомлення. This is the channel of choice if the ТЦК is
  known to be difficult about accepting documents.
- **By e-mail** — to the official address of that ТЦК. Keep the sent item and any delivery receipt.
  Cheap and fast; weakest as proof, so use it *in addition to*, not instead of, one of the above.

Regardless of channel, keep the filed set intact — заява plus every attachment as filed — and
record the filing date in `notes.md`. The five-working-day and 30-day clocks run from registration,
and the whole value of the paper trail is being able to show what was asked and when.
