## Why the Brio 105

1080p with automatic light correction is the whole job for meetings, and the Brio 100/105 is the
cheapest Logitech that does it. At click.ua the 105 costs the same as the 100 and is the one in
stock in graphite, and its shutter is built in rather than a clip-on cap. The C270 saves 500 UAH
for 720p and no cover. The Brio 300 doubles the price for a wider view and a better sensor that
a Meet call mostly throws away.

**Accepted trade-offs:** a **tight 58° view** (on the Brio 100 — the reviewer *"had to adjust
myself to fit in the frame"*); grain in low light; no tripod thread. The 105's own FOV and fps
were not on its vendor page — assumed equal to the 100's, not verified.

**Check before ordering:** nothing — click.ua shows it in stock with a 24-month warranty.
