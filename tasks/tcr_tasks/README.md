# tcr_tasks — mobilisation, military accounting, ТЦК paperwork

Everything related to Dmytro's mobilisation-adjacent administrative matters: applications to
the ТЦК, military-accounting documents, бронювання/відстрочка, and the registers behind them
(Єдиний державний реєстр призовників — «Оберіг» — and Резерв+).

## Subfolders

| Folder | Matter |
|---|---|
| `registry_application/` | Заява to the ТЦК about entering/correcting data in the Реєстр (currently: a бронювання missing from the register) |

Each matter folder holds `notes.md` (what it is, current state), `source/` (documents received
as-is, never edited), `RESEARCH.md` where the rules had to be established, and the drafts.

## Standing cautions for this folder

- **The law here changes monthly.** Every rule in these notes carries the act, its number and
  its date. Before acting on a note older than a few weeks, re-verify against
  `zakon.rada.gov.ua` — several of the governing acts were replaced outright in 2025–2026.
- **Nothing here is legal advice.** These are working notes on procedure. Anything heading
  towards a court goes to a lawyer first.
- Ukrainian text that will be submitted anywhere passes `/style-check` before it is shown.
