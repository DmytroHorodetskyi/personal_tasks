# Lviv city: confirmed ground impacts, 1 Jan 2025 – 25 Sep 2026

Why: choosing a safer district for a friend moving to Lviv.
Scope: Lviv city only (plus settlements under the city council); air strikes only; impacts
confirmed on the ground (hit or falling debris that did damage). Oblast-only events and
interceptions with no damage are listed separately at the end.

Coverage check: the ZAXID.NET «Вибухи у Львові» tag (every entry 29-06-2025 … 13-09-2026) and
the uk.wikipedia chronology «Обстріли Львова та області (з 2022)» were read in full and agree.
No city impact was found for Jan–May 2025.

## Not counted

- 29 Jun 2025 — "critical infrastructure" in Lviv raion, Sadovyi: no civilian building damaged in the city.
- 11 Feb 2026 — «Кинджал» at Lviv, intercepted; debris in Zolochiv and Stryi raions.
- 16 Mar 2026 bus explosion near Галицьке перехрестя, 22 Feb 2026 terror attack in the centre — not air strikes.
- 18 Mar 2026 drone on the SBU directorate — Wikipedia files it under the oblast; location not verified.
- Oblast-only: 24 Jan 2025 Підбірці, 6 Jun 2025 Drohobych, 30 Oct 2025 Добротвір, 6 & 23 Dec 2025 energy, 13 Sep 2026 Stryi.

## Pattern

- Залізничний district (west: Левандівка, Сигнівка, airport, Олени Степанівни, Патона/Виговського)
  took 5–6 of the 10 impacts; its industrial zones and energy sites are the targets, homes nearby get hit.
- Сихів: one strike (24 Mar 2026). Centre: two drone hits (15 Jan, 24 Mar 2026).
- Личаківський and Шевченківський: no confirmed impact found in this period.
- Proximity to industrial parks, warehouses, rail and energy objects predicts risk better than the
  district name.
